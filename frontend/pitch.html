Architecture

Live Qloo API Integration

Real-time queries to https://hackathon.api.qloo.com

1. Entity Search: GET /search?query={term}

Resolves cultural queries (Radiohead, A24, Patagonia) to standardized Qloo UUIDs (e.g. 70CAE5BF-2F4C...).

2. Place Insights: GET /v2/insights (type: place)

Retrieves physical place archetypes & tags: Tranquil, Historic, Parks, Nature conservation.

3. Brand Insights: GET /v2/insights (type: brand)

Retrieves correlated consumer brands: Patagonia, Blinkist, TED Talks, Albion Fit.

Mathematical Modeling

Weighted Euclidean Trait Distance

Predictive compatibility modeling against curated biological traits

The 5 Dimensions (1.0 to 5.0)

  • 🏃 Activity Level (Weight: 1.25): Relaxed pace vs. high cardio.
  • 🏡 Living Space (Weight: 1.20): Compact studio vs. fenced yard.
  • 🤝 Social Volume (Weight: 1.00): Quiet solitude vs. party host.
  • ⏱️ Home Routine (Weight: 1.00): Frequent flyer vs. 100% remote.
  • 🔊 Noise Tolerance (Weight: 0.90): Pin-drop quiet vs. lively soundscape.

Distance & Compatibility Equation

D(b) = √ [ Σ w_i · ( L_i - T_b,i )² ]

Match % = max( 50, (1 - D(b) / D_max) × 100 )

Explainable AI: The engine isolates the closest trait dimensions to generate personalized “Why It Fits Your Vibe” justifications.
